# Phase 272 - Agreements You Can Read Again

The agreements a new server asks you to accept were written early and had not kept up with the product. They
said little about what happens when a licence ends, nothing about hosting other people, and the privacy text
was vaguer than it should have been. We rewrote all of them under UK law, in plain language.

## What changed

**Terms of Service for the software.** They now cover the licence and what it allows, the free trial, what
happens when a licence lapses (your websites, email and DNS keep running throughout), updates and support,
what you are responsible for, and what each side is liable for. Consumers and businesses are treated
separately, as the law requires.

**Privacy Policy.** It says exactly what the panel sends to us and what it never does, so you can judge it
for yourself.

**Acceptable Use Policy.** New. It says what is not allowed, what is expected of anyone who hosts other
people, and where to report abuse.

**The website matches.** The privacy, terms, security and acceptable use pages on the website were rewritten
to agree with the panel's, and each now says where to take a complaint about how something was handled.

## Readable at any time

The agreements are shown once, during setup, which is when nobody reads them. Administrators can now open
them again from links in the Controller footer, with the date they were accepted. Resellers and customers do
not see the links, because the agreements are between the server's owner and us.
