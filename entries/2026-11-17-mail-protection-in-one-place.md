# Phase 293 - Mail Protection In One Place

Five separate pages each held a switch and a handful of numbers: spam filtering, the settings for the spam checker itself,
virus scanning, greylisting, and the block lists. None of them said whether the thing the switch controlled was actually
running, so a protection could be on and doing nothing, with no sign anywhere. And one more page held a single button for
repairing mailbox permissions.

## What changed

The five are now tabs of one page, Mail Protection, with an overview first. A strip across the top shows whether the spam
checker, the virus scanner and the greylisting service are running. If a protection is switched on and its helper has stopped,
the page says so in red and the administrator gets an alert, because mail still arrives but nothing is checking it. The
overview also counts what the protections have done lately, from the mail log, without reading or keeping a single address.

Each setting now says what it does and the risky ones warn as you make them: refusing spam outright, throwing infected mail away
without telling anyone, blocking a whole country. The repair button moved into Mail Settings, where it belongs.

Email Archiving was rebuilt around the fact that it keeps a copy of other people's mail. Turning it on now asks the
administrator to confirm they have a lawful reason and have told the people whose mail it is, and that is recorded. The list
no longer shows subjects, addresses are shortened, and opening a message needs a recorded reason.
