# Phase 311 - Three Pages, One Brand

Web Templates was two boxes of raw HTML, one for the page on a new domain and one for a suspended account, and asking
someone to write a web page to change a sentence was the wrong deal. A third page, shown when a security scan suspends an
account, could not be changed at all, and a change to the suspended page only reached accounts suspended afterwards.

## What changed

All three pages are now shown as visitors see them, and each is edited as wording: a heading, a message and a small label,
with a live preview that includes a phone view. Colour, light or dark, company name and a contact line are set once and
apply to all three, so they match. The logo is the KNJ logo, shown exactly as it is, until your own is uploaded.

Writing your own HTML is still there for anyone who wants it, kept out of the way. A change now reaches accounts that are
suspended at that moment.
